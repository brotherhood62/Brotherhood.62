## About MeetHearts 
MeetHearts is a project focused on bringing people together at physical venues to build meaningful personal connections, friendships, and brotherhood. 

### Meet-up Venues 
For physical gatherings and community meet-ups, we can organize activities across different venues such as **indoor sports facilities, food points, parks, and educational institutions**. 

### Policies 
- Contribution will not be accepted from attendees for 24 hours after the gathering or meeting.
